## AgriEqub 
An innovative digital platform that combines the trusted Ethiopian Equb (rotating savings and credit groups) with end-to-end coffee farm traceability and EUDR compliance tools.

# The goal is to solve real challenges I’ve heard from farmers and unions:

Informal Equb groups often face trust and record-keeping issues.
Manual traceability and EUDR compliance is time-consuming and expensive for small co-ops.
Farmers struggle to prove their good practices to get better prices and access to credit.

AgriEqub Trace bridges traditional trust-based Equb with modern export requirements — all while keeping the solution simple, mobile-first, and built for rural Ethiopia.
